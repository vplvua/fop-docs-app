## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Skip payment action

The system SHALL provide a server action to mark a payment as `skipped`. No act SHALL be generated by skipping, and a `skipped` payment SHALL NOT be reclassifiable through automatic or manual classification. A `skipped` payment MAY still be resolved by **manually splitting it into acts** (see the `payment-split` capability), which transitions it to `classified` — skip is terminal for classification but not for splitting.

Covers: FR-CLASS-18.

#### Scenario: Skip a payment

- **WHEN** the admin skips a payment with `status = in_queue`
- **THEN** `payment.status` SHALL be `skipped`

#### Scenario: Skip is terminal for classification

- **WHEN** a payment has `status = skipped`
- **THEN** the classify action SHALL NOT be available

#### Scenario: A skipped payment can still be split

- **WHEN** a payment has `status = skipped` and the admin splits it into acts whose amounts sum to the payment amount
- **THEN** the acts SHALL be created, linked to the payment, and the payment SHALL transition to `classified`

### Requirement: Payment card shows classification actions

The payment detail page (`/payments/[id]`) SHALL display an action panel: for `received`/`awaiting_review`/`in_queue` — "Класифікувати", "Пропустити", and "Розділити на акти" actions; for `in_queue` — the classification reason with contextual guidance; for `classified` — a read-only link to the created act, or, when the payment backs more than one act (a split), links to **all** of them; for `skipped` — a "Пропущено" badge and a "Розділити на акти" action. For `awaiting_review` with `classification_reason` containing `multiple_clients_same_edrpou`, the panel SHALL show a warning that several active clients share the payer EDRPOU and a selector listing only those active clients; selecting one SHALL link the payment to that client and continue classification.

#### Scenario: Action panel for in_queue payment

- **WHEN** the admin views a payment with `status = in_queue` and `classification_reason` containing `no_match`
- **THEN** the page SHALL show "Класифікувати", "Пропустити", and "Розділити на акти" actions, and guidance text explaining the payment could not be matched to a client

#### Scenario: Action panel for classified single-act payment

- **WHEN** the admin views a payment with `status = classified` backed by exactly one act
- **THEN** the page SHALL show a read-only summary with a link to that act

#### Scenario: Action panel for split (multi-act) payment

- **WHEN** the admin views a payment with `status = classified` backed by more than one act
- **THEN** the page SHALL list links to all of the linked acts with their amounts

#### Scenario: Action panel for skipped payment

- **WHEN** the admin views a payment with `status = skipped`
- **THEN** the page SHALL show a "Пропущено" badge and a "Розділити на акти" action

#### Scenario: Selector for multiple clients with same EDRPOU

- **WHEN** the admin views a payment with `status = awaiting_review` and `classification_reason` containing `multiple_clients_same_edrpou`
- **THEN** the page SHALL show a warning and a selector of the active clients sharing the payer EDRPOU, and archived clients SHALL NOT be listed
